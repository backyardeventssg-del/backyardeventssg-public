Common questions

Quick answers before you book. Anything else, just message us.

How long can I book a booth for?

Booths are booked for either a weekend or a full week. Each event's details show which lengths are available and the price for each.

How much will it cost?

Every event shows its booth prices upfront, for a weekend or a full week, and the booking form shows your total before you send it.

How do I reserve a booth?

Open an event, choose your booth type and booking length, and send us your details. Then confirm on WhatsApp and we'll complete your booking with you there.

How do I pay?

Payment is confirmed on WhatsApp. After you send your request, we'll share the PayNow details with you there, and your booth is reserved once we confirm your payment.

What's included with my booth?

It depends on the event. Open any event to see what's provided, along with the floor plan and photos of the space.

Can I book more than one booth?

Yes, you can request several booths in one booking. For larger setups, message us and we'll help you plan it.

Will my products suit your markets?

Our markets are curated around sustainability, handmade and festive themes. If you're not sure whether your products fit, message us and we'll let you know.
